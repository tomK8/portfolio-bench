Highlighted rows sit on the wrong side of their own limit — a buy whose limit is at or above the market, or a sell at or below it. A resting order in that state should already have executed, so either the Current price is stale (nothing keeps an order-only symbol's feed warm between ticks) or the broker export pre-dates the fill.

Rows marked pre-market / after-hours are priced off an extended-hours quote — used because it's the newest price available, and the alternative is a close that's hours older. Those rows never raise the amber through-limit flag: a resting regular-hours order doesn't fill outside regular hours, so sitting through the limit on a thin pre-market print isn't the same signal.

~ II doesn't export the resting limit price, so it's estimated as order value ÷ quantity. It's slightly off — the ~£2.99 dealing fee (and, on USD orders, an FX charge levied in GBP) is folded into the order value.
