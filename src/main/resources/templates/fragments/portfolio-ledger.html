Annualised return is an IRR over each position's dated cash flows (buys negative and inclusive of fees, sells net, dividends on their pay date, current market value as a terminal flow dated today), solved in GBP so FX is inside the figure. Positions are accumulated in chunks, so there is no single holding period to annualise over and no CAGR to compute; IRR needs no start date. A position sold to zero and re-entered (↻) is reported for its current episode only, since flows that change sign repeatedly can admit several rates. A span shorter than 90 days shows the raw period return with its length beside it (41d) rather than an annualised figure.

Group rows sum Market value, Portfolio %, Gain, Dividends and Total gain, and weight Gain % and Total gain % as Σgain ÷ Σcost — the return the money in the group actually earned, not the average of the member percentages, which a small position with a large percentage would otherwise dominate. Quantity, Price, Ccy and Sources are blank because they have no meaning across different instruments, and Annualised return is blank because it has no correct group-level value: members hold for different periods over different flow histories, so no weighting of their IRRs is defensible. The right answer is one money-weighted IRR over the union of the group's dated cash flows — a different calculation over the ledger rows rather than these aggregated ones — and it is tracked as its own piece of work rather than approximated here. Every holding lands in exactly one group, so the group values add back to the total above.
